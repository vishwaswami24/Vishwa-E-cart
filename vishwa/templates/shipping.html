SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

SHIPPING METHODS

We offer multiple shipping options to meet your needs:

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days
  • Overnight Shipping: Next business day (major cities only)

SHIPPING COSTS

Shipping costs are calculated based on:

  • Order weight and dimensions
  • Delivery location
  • Selected shipping method
  • Orders over ₹500 qualify for free standard shipping

ORDER PROCESSING

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once your order ships.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

We currently ship within India only. International shipping may be available in the future.

DELIVERY ISSUES

If you experience any issues with delivery:

  • Contact our customer service within 48 hours
  • Provide your order number and tracking information
  • We will work to resolve the issue promptly

DAMAGED OR MISSING ITEMS

If your package arrives damaged or items are missing:

  • Report the issue within 24 hours of delivery
  • Take photos of the damaged packaging and items
  • We will arrange for replacement or refund
FREE SHIPPING

Enjoy free standard shipping on orders over ₹500!
